# TaskFlow AI — Employee Handbook

## Welcome to TaskFlow AI

This handbook outlines the policies, procedures, and guidelines for all employees.
Please read this document carefully and reach out to HR if you have any questions.

## 1. Working Hours

Standard office hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday through Friday.
Flexible working arrangements are available with manager approval.
Remote work is permitted for up to two days per week for eligible roles.

## 2. Leave Policy

### Annual Leave
Full-time employees are entitled to 20 days of annual leave per year.
Leave requests must be submitted at least two weeks in advance.
Unused leave of up to 10 days may be carried forward to the next year.

### Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of paid sick leave per year.
A medical certificate is required for absences longer than three consecutive days.

### Public Holidays
Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays.

## 3. Code of Conduct

All employees must maintain professional conduct at all times.
Harassment, discrimination, or bullying of any kind will not be tolerated.
Violations will result in disciplinary action up to and including termination.

## 4. Data Privacy and Security

Employees must handle all company and customer data in accordance with the
Data Protection Policy. Sharing confidential information externally without
authorization is a serious disciplinary offence.

Employees must:
- Use strong, unique passwords for all company systems.
- Enable two-factor authentication where available.
- Lock their workstations when stepping away.
- Report any suspected security breaches immediately to IT.

## 5. Performance Reviews

Performance reviews are conducted twice per year — in June and December.
Employees will receive a written evaluation from their direct manager.
Salary adjustments, if any, are effective from the January following the review.

## 6. Training and Development

TaskFlow AI supports continuous learning. Employees are entitled to:
- Annual training budget of $500 for courses, books, and conferences.
- Internal mentorship programme access.
- Quarterly lunch-and-learn sessions on emerging technologies.

## 7. Expense Reimbursement

Business expenses must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the cost.
Receipts are required for all expenses above $25.
Expenses are reimbursed via payroll on the last working day of each month.

## 8. IT and Equipment

The company provides a laptop and necessary peripherals to all employees.
Personal use of company equipment is permitted within reason.
All company devices must have approved security software installed.

## 9. Disciplinary Procedure

The company follows a progressive disciplinary process:
1. Verbal warning (documented).
2. Written warning.
3. Final written warning.
4. Termination.

Serious misconduct may result in immediate termination without prior warning.

## 10. Grievance Procedure

Employees who have a grievance should first discuss it with their direct manager.
If unresolved, the matter may be escalated to HR.
Formal grievances must be submitted in writing.
