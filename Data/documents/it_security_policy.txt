IT SECURITY POLICY
TaskFlow AI — Information Technology Security Policy
Version 3.0 | Effective Date: 2025-01-01

1. PURPOSE AND SCOPE

This policy establishes minimum security requirements for all information technology
systems, networks, and data at TaskFlow AI. It applies to all employees, contractors,
and third-party vendors who access company systems.

2. PASSWORD REQUIREMENTS

All system passwords must meet the following criteria:
- Minimum length: 12 characters.
- Must include uppercase and lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters.
- Must not contain the user's name, email, or common dictionary words.
- Must be changed every 90 days.
- Previous 12 passwords cannot be reused.

Passwords must never be:
- Written down or stored in plain text.
- Shared with colleagues, managers, or IT staff.
- Entered in the presence of others.

3. MULTI-FACTOR AUTHENTICATION (MFA)

MFA is mandatory for:
- All corporate email accounts.
- VPN access.
- Cloud service provider consoles.
- Code repositories.
- Financial and HR systems.

4. DATA CLASSIFICATION

All company data is classified into one of four categories:

PUBLIC: Information intended for external release. No restrictions.
INTERNAL: General business information. Must not be shared externally without approval.
CONFIDENTIAL: Sensitive business data (financials, personnel files). Encrypted storage required.
RESTRICTED: Highest sensitivity (passwords, cryptographic keys). Strictly need-to-know basis.

5. ACCEPTABLE USE OF COMPANY ASSETS

Employees may use company-provided devices for limited personal use provided:
- It does not interfere with work duties.
- It does not involve illegal content.
- It does not consume excessive bandwidth.
- Security software is not disabled.

Installation of unauthorised software is prohibited. Contact IT to request software installation.

6. REMOTE WORK SECURITY

Employees working remotely must:
- Use the company VPN for all business activities.
- Ensure their home Wi-Fi network is password-protected with WPA2 or WPA3.
- Not use public Wi-Fi for business purposes without VPN.
- Keep physical devices secure and out of sight when not in use.

7. INCIDENT REPORTING

Security incidents must be reported to IT Security within one hour of detection.
An incident includes:
- Suspected phishing or social engineering attempt.
- Loss or theft of a company device.
- Unauthorised access to systems or data.
- Malware detection.

Failure to report a known incident is a disciplinary offence.

8. THIRD-PARTY ACCESS

Vendors and contractors requiring access to company systems must:
- Sign the Third-Party Security Agreement.
- Use separate, time-limited credentials.
- Access only the minimum systems necessary for their engagement.
- Return or destroy all company data upon engagement completion.

9. BACKUP AND RECOVERY

Critical business data must be backed up daily.
Backups are retained for a minimum of 90 days.
Recovery procedures are tested quarterly.
Employees must not store critical data exclusively on local devices.

10. COMPLIANCE AND ENFORCEMENT

Violations of this policy may result in disciplinary action up to and including
termination of employment and referral to law enforcement where applicable.
IT Security conducts quarterly audits to verify compliance.
